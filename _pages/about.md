<div style="text-align: justify">   

<p>
  I am a <strong>research scientist and hydroacoustics researcher</strong> at the <strong><a href="http://www.imarpe.gob.pe/" target="_blank">Marine Institute of Peru (IMARPE)</a></strong>, specializing in the intersection of <strong>Data Science, Quantitative Ecology, and Marine Biology</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  I am deeply involved in interdisciplinary research that bridges the gap between complex computational analysis and marine ecosystems. My work focuses on developing <strong>deep learning and machine learning methodologies</strong> to leverage large volumes of acoustic and environmental data, allowing us to extract hidden patterns and better understand population dynamics. 
</p>
<p>
  I am strongly committed to driving the evolution of <strong>hydroacoustics and advanced statistics</strong> to innovate how we monitor, assess, and manage our marine resources.
</p>
